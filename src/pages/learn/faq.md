## Is this website a lender?

No. Free SBA Info is **not a lender and not a commercial loan broker**. We provide free information and an optional referral service.

## Does the SBA give me the money?

Usually no. Participating lenders fund the loan and the SBA guarantees part of it.

## What is the maximum loan amount?

$5 million for a standard 7(a) loan.

## Can I get a 7(a) loan with a startup?

Yes, some startups qualify, but lenders usually want strong personal credit, relevant experience, a solid business plan, and a meaningful owner investment.

## What credit score do I need?

The SBA does not set a single minimum. Many lenders look for personal scores in the high 600s or higher. Requirements vary.

## Is collateral required?

Lenders generally take available collateral for larger loans, but a loan can still be approved if collateral is limited. See [collateral and guarantees](/learn/collateral-and-guarantees/).

## How long does approval take?

From a few weeks to a few months.

## Does the quiz collect my personal information?

No. The quiz asks only about your business situation and never your name, business name, or contact details. Answers stay in your browser.

## What happens if I ask for a lender referral?

You will be asked for contact information and your explicit permission. We then share it, along with your quiz answers, with SBA lenders we have relationships with. Using the referral is optional and has no cost to you. See our [disclosures](/disclosures/).

## Does a "likely eligible" result mean I'm approved?

No. Only a lender can approve a loan, after reviewing your full application.
