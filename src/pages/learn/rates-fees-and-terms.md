## Interest rates

The SBA sets **maximum** interest rates. Lenders may charge less. Rates are quoted as a base rate (such as the Prime Rate) plus a spread. Larger loans generally have smaller maximum spreads.

| Loan amount | Typical maximum spread over base rate |
|---|---|
| $50,000 or less | Base + 6.5% |
| $50,001 to $250,000 | Base + 6.0% |
| $250,001 to $350,000 | Base + 4.5% |
| Over $350,000 | Base + 3.0% |

Rates may be **fixed or variable**. Variable rates adjust over the life of the loan.

> **Verify current figures.** The SBA updates these maximums periodically. Confirm the current numbers at [sba.gov](https://www.sba.gov/funding-programs/loans/7a-loans) before relying on them.

## Fees

- **SBA guarantee fee**: a one-time fee based on the guaranteed portion and the loan size and term. The SBA changes this schedule from time to time, and some loan sizes may have reduced or waived fees.
- **Lender fees**: packaging, closing, or servicing fees, within SBA limits
- **No prepayment penalty** on most loans with terms under 15 years. Longer-term loans may have a declining penalty during the first three years.

## Repayment terms

| Purpose | Maximum term |
|---|---|
| Working capital, equipment (based on useful life), most purposes | Up to 10 years |
| Commercial real estate | Up to 25 years |

Because terms are long, monthly payments are often lower than on shorter conventional loans.

## What affects your actual rate

Your lender will consider your credit history, cash flow, collateral, industry, and the size of the loan. Comparing offers from more than one lender is smart.
