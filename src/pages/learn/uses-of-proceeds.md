7(a) loans are flexible. Funds must be used for a **sound business purpose**.

## Common approved uses

- **Working capital**: payroll, inventory, and day-to-day operating expenses
- **Equipment and machinery**: purchase or installation
- **Commercial real estate**: buying land or a building, or constructing or renovating (the business must generally occupy a majority of owner-occupied space)
- **Leasehold improvements**
- **Buying a business** or a change of ownership, including partner buyouts
- **Starting a business**: startup costs
- **Refinancing** existing business debt, when the refinance gives a clear benefit to the business
- **Franchise fees** (for franchises on the SBA's eligible list)

## Uses that are not allowed

- Personal expenses or **distributions to owners** not tied to a business purpose
- **Speculative** investments
- Paying certain **delinquent taxes** (such as unpaid payroll trust-fund taxes)
- Refinancing debt that the SBA does not allow to be refinanced, or debt that already has an SBA guarantee in some cases
- Purchasing real estate for **passive investment**

## Your lender decides the details

Each lender will ask for a **use-of-proceeds** breakdown in your application. Having this ready makes the process faster. See [how to apply](/learn/how-to-apply/).
