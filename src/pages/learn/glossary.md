**7(a) loan**: the SBA's flagship loan program, made by approved lenders with an SBA guarantee.

**Collateral**: assets pledged to a lender to secure a loan.

**Debt service coverage ratio (DSCR)**: your business's cash flow divided by its loan payments. Lenders usually want it above 1.0, often 1.15 or higher.

**Equity injection**: cash the owner puts into a purchase or startup.

**Guarantee (SBA)**: the SBA's promise to repay a lender a percentage of a loss if the borrower defaults.

**Guarantee fee**: a fee charged on the guaranteed portion of an SBA loan.

**Personal guarantee**: a promise by an owner to repay the loan personally if the business cannot.

**Prime Rate**: a benchmark interest rate often used as the base for variable-rate loans.

**Size standard**: the SBA's limit, by industry, on revenue or employees for a business to be considered small.

**Term loan**: a loan repaid in installments over a fixed period.

**Use of proceeds**: how you plan to spend the loan funds.

**Working capital**: money used for day-to-day operations.
