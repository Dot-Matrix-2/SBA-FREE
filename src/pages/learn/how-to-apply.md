## Step by step

1. **Know your numbers.** Decide how much you need and exactly how you will use it.
2. **Check eligibility.** Take the [anonymous quiz](/quiz/) and read the [eligibility guide](/learn/eligibility/).
3. **Gather documents** (checklist below).
4. **Choose a lender.** You apply directly with an SBA-approved lender. Talk to more than one and compare.
5. **Complete the application.** The lender helps with the required SBA forms.
6. **Underwriting.** The lender reviews your credit, cash flow, and collateral. Some lenders can approve under their own SBA delegated authority, which is generally faster.
7. **Closing and funding.** After approval you sign loan documents and funds are disbursed.

## Document checklist

**For the business**

- Business tax returns, typically the last 3 years
- Year-to-date profit and loss statement and balance sheet
- Debt schedule listing current business debts
- Business plan and financial projections (especially for startups)
- Business license and formation documents
- Lease or real estate documents, if relevant
- Quotes or invoices for equipment or other purchases

**For each owner of 20% or more**

- Personal tax returns, typically the last 3 years
- Personal financial statement
- Resume or business experience summary
- Government-issued ID

## How long does it take?

It varies widely, from a few weeks to a few months, depending on the lender, loan size, and how complete your documents are.

## Where to get free help

[SBA Small Business Development Centers](https://www.sba.gov/local-assistance/resource-partners/small-business-development-centers-sbdc), [SCORE](https://www.score.org/) mentors, and [Women's Business Centers](https://www.sba.gov/local-assistance/resource-partners/womens-business-centers) offer free or low-cost counseling.
