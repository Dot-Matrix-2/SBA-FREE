## Summary

- You can read this entire site and take the quiz **without giving us any personal information**.
- Quiz answers stay in your browser unless you choose the optional lender referral.
- If you request a referral, we share your information with SBA lenders, as described below.

## Information we collect

**Quiz answers.** Processed in your browser. Not sent to us unless you request a referral.

**Referral information (optional).** If you ask to be referred to lenders, we collect your name, business name, email, phone number, state, your quiz answers, and a record of your consent (the wording you agreed to and the time).

**Technical data.** Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes standard request data such as IP address and browser type to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. We use Cloudflare Turnstile to block spam on the referral form.

## How we use and share it

We use referral information to pass it to SBA lenders with whom we have relationships so they can contact you about loan options. We do not sell your information to other third parties. Lenders that receive your information handle it under their own privacy policies.

## Retention

We keep referral records for as long as needed for business, legal, and compliance purposes, then delete or anonymize them.

## Your choices

You may ask us to delete your information, or ask what we hold, by contacting us at the address below. Because lenders receive your information, you may also need to contact them directly. You can opt out of marketing communications from a lender by following its instructions.

## Cookies

We do not use advertising cookies. Turnstile may set limited cookies when the referral form is shown.

## Children

This site is intended for adults who own or operate businesses.
