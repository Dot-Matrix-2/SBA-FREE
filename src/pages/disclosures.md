## Not a lender or broker

Free SBA Info is not a lender and not a commercial loan broker. We do not originate, fund, arrange, or negotiate loans, and we do not guarantee that you will be contacted by, approved by, or receive an offer from any lender.

## Not affiliated with the SBA

This website is independent. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the U.S. Small Business Administration or any government agency.

## Quiz results

The eligibility quiz is a general educational tool based on published SBA criteria. Results are not a credit decision, an offer of credit, or a prediction of approval. Only an SBA-approved lender can evaluate and approve an application.

## Lender referrals and compensation

If you opt in, we share the information you provide with SBA lenders with whom we have relationships. **We may receive compensation from those lenders** for referrals. This does not increase your costs. Lenders in our network are not the only options available, and you are encouraged to compare offers from other lenders.

## No advice

Content on this site is for general information only and is not legal, tax, accounting, or financial advice. Consult qualified professionals about your situation.

## Accuracy

We work to keep information current but cannot guarantee that it is complete or up to date. SBA rules, rates, and fees change.
