Free SBA Info exists to help U.S. small business owners understand SBA 7(a) term loans and find out whether they may qualify, without cost and without having to share personal information.

## What we are, and are not

- We **are** an educational website with a free, anonymous eligibility quiz.
- We are **not a lender** and **not a commercial loan broker**. We do not make loans, take applications, negotiate terms, or make credit decisions.
- We are **not affiliated with the U.S. Small Business Administration**.

## Why is it free?

You never pay to use this site. If you choose the optional lender referral at the end of the quiz, SBA lenders that we have relationships with may compensate us for introductions. That compensation does not affect what you pay for a loan, and you are never required to use it. See our [disclosures](/disclosures/).

## Our content

Our guides are based on public information from the SBA. Each page shows when it was last reviewed. Program rules change, so please confirm details at [sba.gov](https://www.sba.gov/funding-programs/loans/7a-loans) or with a lender before making decisions.
